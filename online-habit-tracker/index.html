Tap a square: once for done, twice for skipped, three times to clear.

How it works

  1. A tap a day

    Tap a square once for done and twice for skipped; a third tap clears it. Skipped days do not break the streak.

  2. It stays in this browser

    Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. Your habits are saved on this device, in this browser, until you delete them or clear your browsing data.

  3. See the year, export it

    The Year view shows each habit as a full year of squares. Export PNG draws it on white, ready to share or print.
