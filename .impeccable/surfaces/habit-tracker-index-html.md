# Quilt web: printable habit tracker 2027 (and its maker and comparisons)

Scope: `habit-tracker/` (Persuade: free printables, en), with `es/habit-tracker-para-imprimir/`, `pt/planilha-de-habitos/`, the makers under them (Operate) and the comparison pages (Read), all in one world.
Audience: people printing a habit tracker for 2027 in late December and January; people leaving another habit app.
Job: download the PDF (or open the maker) in seconds; see that a year of squares per habit is Quilt's idea; install the app if paper is not enough.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a quilt being pieced. Every region is a cotton patch sewn to the next with a visible running stitch, and the year grids are the print on the patches. It refuses the pastel bullet-journal printable site (washi tape, doodles, cards in a row) and the split SaaS hero.

OWN-WORLD: Cream backing `#FBF8F3` (dark `#17150F`), the app's eight pastel cottons as solid fields that own whole regions, warm ink `#2E2A24` on them, seams drawn as dashed running stitch in ink at about a third, corners slightly rounded like pressed fabric, a woven-label chip for small meta text. Display type Caprasimo (soft chunky 70s quilt-shop letters), body in the system sans. Dark mode keeps the fields as deep dyed versions of the same cottons.

STORY: The visitor sees a real 2027 sheet with the whole year as squares, picks layout and paper, downloads; understands Quilt fills the same quilt on the phone; may open the maker or the app.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One patchwork block across the wrap. Left patch (butter) holds H1 with a big 2027, the lede, layout and paper choices and the Download PDF button; right patch (cream, stitched inside its edge) holds the sheet preview at true paper ratio; a thin mint strip under the block names three things on the sheet. On phones the patches stack as strips in the same order.

FORM: Patchwork quilt, the user's pick (impeccable's top grounded candidate, second-ranked in the dealt order), seed bf48d8bb. Raises kept from the declined challengers: from the step sequencer, today always marked and an empty row read as an invitation; from the ANSI board, every grid aligned to the day cell so nothing floats off the weave; from the obi sleeve, one bare field set against one dense band (the sheet patch against the crowded controls patch).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: marking a day in the maker sews the patch (fill, then the stitch outline draws), and tapping again marks it skipped, then clears it.
