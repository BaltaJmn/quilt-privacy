A HabitKit alternative with free widgets and skipped days

HabitKit and Quilt are close: both draw your year as a grid of squares, both work on Android and iPhone, and neither needs an account. They differ in what is free, in skipped days and in usage analytics. HabitKit gives you 4 free habits; Quilt gives you 3.

Checked on October 26, 2026

Quilt fits if

  • You want home screen widgets without paying: every widget in Quilt is free.
  • You want skipped days that do not break a streak, and export to CSV as well as JSON.
  • You want an app with no usage analytics: Quilt makes no connection except to process a purchase.

HabitKit fits if

  • You keep 4 habits and want all of them free.
  • You want Count and Measure habits with units and decimals, "Stay Below" limits and categories.
  • You want a daily iCloud backup on iPhone.

Where HabitKit is the better pick

  • 4 free habits

    HabitKit lets you keep 4 habits free for as long as you like, one more than Quilt, along with Yes/No, Count and Measure habits, notes, categories, quit habits and three dashboard views.

  • Count and Measure with units

    Habits that count or measure with units and decimals, "Stay Below" limits for habits you want to quit, categories, share cards, more than 30 colors and hundreds of icons.

  • iCloud backup

    On iPhone, HabitKit backs up to iCloud every day, and it keeps 7 daily backups on the device. In Quilt you export a file yourself.

Questions

Is HabitKit free?

Yes, up to 4 habits, for as long as you like and with no trial clock. The free plan includes Yes/No, Count and Measure habits, notes, categories, quit habits, one reminder per habit, backups and export. Pro adds unlimited habits, home screen widgets, charts and statistics, up to three reminders per habit and dashboard customization. It is sold monthly, yearly or once as Lifetime; the store shows the current price.

Is there a HabitKit alternative with free widgets?

Quilt: every widget is free, in three sizes, on Android and iPhone; on iPhone also on the lock screen, and on Android there is a quick settings tile. Free is 3 habits, one fewer than HabitKit. Pro is $1.99 once or $0.99 a month and adds unlimited habits and the full color palette.

Does HabitKit have skipped days?

Not as a feature. Its help center says it has no dedicated skip-day, rest-day or streak-freeze feature, and suggests weekly or monthly goals, or no streak goal, as workarounds. In Quilt you press and hold a day to skip it; it counts as neither a miss nor a win, and your streak and percentage stay as they were.

Can I move my HabitKit data to Quilt?

Not automatically. HabitKit exports one JSON file, and Quilt imports only its own JSON backup, so it cannot read HabitKit's file. To bring a year over, tap the days on Quilt's grid.

Try Quilt for a week

Add the habits you track now and tick a week. If it is not for you, there is no account to close and nothing to cancel.
