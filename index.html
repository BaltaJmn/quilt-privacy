Quilt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

English - Español - Terms of use - Legal notice

The short version. Quilt works without an account. Your habits, your history and your streaks are stored only on your device and are never uploaded. There is an account only if you choose to share a habit with friends (optional, described below): then, and only then, that one habit and the days you did it are kept on our server. The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, which receives some data from your device (see "Ads (free version)" below); Quilt Pro removes it. On iPhone, if you see ads, iOS asks whether Quilt may track you, and you can say no. Apart from the ads and shared habits, the only data that leaves your device is what is needed to know whether you have bought Quilt Pro. We have no analytics of our own.

Who is responsible

Quilt (“the app”) is published by Baltasar Jiménez (“we”, “us”), an individual developer based in Spain, who acts as data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact for anything in this policy: baltajmn@gmail.com. The full details of the publisher are in the legal notice.

What stays on your device

Everything you actually create in Quilt is stored locally, in a file inside the app’s private storage, and in a shared container that lets the home-screen widget read it:

  • The habits you create: name, emoji, colour, schedule and daily target.
  • Your history: which days you completed, how many times, and which days you marked as skipped.
  • Your reminder times.
  • Whether you have purchased Quilt Pro.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else, with one exception that you choose: a habit you share with friends (see below). Uninstalling the app deletes it from your phone.

What leaves your device

Purchases

Quilt offers Quilt Pro, as a one-time purchase or as a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it in your store settings. Payments are processed by the store you installed Quilt from, Google Play or the App Store; we never see or receive your card details, billing address or any other payment information.

To know whether a given installation has bought Quilt Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat assigns each installation an anonymous identifier and stores, on our behalf, that identifier, the purchase history associated with it, and technical device identifiers. Because Quilt has no accounts, this identifier is not linked to your name, your email address or any profile. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

RevenueCat processes data in the United States. Transfers outside the European Economic Area are covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Sharing a habit with friends (optional account)

Everything above works without an account. Only sharing a habit with other people asks you to sign in, with Sign in with Apple or Google, and only when you open that part of the app. If you never do, nothing in this section applies to you.

When you use it we store, on our server: the identifier and the email address that Apple or Google give us when you sign in (with Apple you may choose a private relay address), the name you choose to show, your friend code, your friends and the people you block, and the reports you send. For each habit you share or join we store its name, emoji, schedule and target, the people in it and, for each of them, the days they did it over the last eight weeks. Only the people in a shared habit can see it, and they see your chosen name and which days you did it. Your other habits, your history, notes, reminders and statistics stay on your phone: sharing never sends them.

The server is run by Supabase, Inc. as a processor, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). The same account system is shared with Chroma, FlowTime and MoodTraker, other apps by the same developer: if you sign in to several of them with the same Apple or Google account, it is one account, but each app keeps its own name, friends and data, and no app can see another's.

There is zero tolerance for abusive names and habits. You can report or block anyone from the app, and a report sends us an email with the names of the two people involved so that we can act within 24 hours; it is delivered by Resend, Inc. as a processor. See the terms of use.

So that Quilt can tell you when someone invites you to a habit or joins yours, also with the app closed, we store a notification token for your phone with your account. It is handed to Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, on Android) or Apple (Apple Push Notification service, on iPhone) to deliver each notification, together with its text, which includes the chosen name of the person and the name of the shared habit. The token is deleted when you sign out or delete your account. If you turn notifications off for Quilt, nothing is shown.

You can change your name, leave a habit, remove people from a habit you started, block and unblock people, sign out and delete your account at any time from the app (Settings, Friends, Delete account; also at quilt.baltajmn.dev/delete.html). Deleting it removes your name, friends, blocks and your place in every shared habit, with the days you marked there, from the server at once. If you also use another app of ours with the same account, that app's account is kept until you delete it there.

Ads (free version)

The free version of Quilt shows ads from Google AdMob: a single banner. There are no full-screen ads. Quilt Pro, bought once or monthly, removes the banner. If you were already using Quilt before ads were added, you never see it; on a new installation it appears from the third day. Quilt asks Google to show only ads that are suitable for all ages (content rating G).

When ads are shown, Google's ads software on your phone collects and receives the following, for advertising, analytics and to prevent fraud, and shares it with Google:

  • identifiers of your device, such as the advertising ID (on iPhone, the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking: see "Tracking permission on iPhone" below);
  • how you interact with the ads and the app;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app and the ads software;
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address.

Quilt does not give the ads software your habits, your history or your settings: the only habit Quilt ever sends anywhere is one you choose to share with friends, and it goes to our own server, not to Google. Google handles what it receives as an independent controller, under its own policies: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-US framework); Google's terms add Standard Contractual Clauses where that does not apply. Google is the only ad network Quilt uses.

Consent in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. There, Quilt shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) and does not request ads until you have answered it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy options, which appears only where a form applies. Settings also has an option to report an inappropriate ad.

Google is contacted when the app starts. For people who see ads, the consent software contacts Google every time the app starts, to find out whether a form applies to you and to keep your choice up to date. Like any request to Google, this reveals your IP address and the app's details to Google, also where no form is shown.

Tracking permission on iPhone. On iPhone, after the consent form (where it applies) and before the first ad, iOS shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, which asks whether Quilt may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is available to Google's ads software, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not available and you still see ads, without it. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Android advertising ID. Google's ads software reads your advertising ID, which is why Quilt declares the normal permission "AD_ID". You can reset or delete the ID at any time in your phone's Android settings; apps then get a string of zeros instead of the ID.

Nothing else

Quilt contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of our own. The other software that collects data is the purchases library (RevenueCat), the connection to our server if you share a habit, and Google's ads software in the free version, all described above; the ads software also uses what it collects for its own analytics and fraud prevention. We do not build profiles, we do not use remarketing, and apart from the ads and the services described above we do not share personal information with anyone. The app needs an internet connection only to check your purchase status, to load the ads in the free version and, if you use it, to share habits with friends.

Files you export

Quilt can export your data as a backup file or as a CSV spreadsheet. You choose where that file is saved. Once saved, the file is yours and is outside our control. If you put it in a cloud folder, that provider’s policy applies to it, not this one.

How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete the habit or uninstall the app. Purchase records held by RevenueCat and by the app store are kept for as long as those services require in order to honour restores and to meet their own legal and tax obligations. Account data is kept until you delete your account. The days of a shared habit are kept for the last eight weeks and older ones are erased every night; a person who leaves a shared habit takes their days with them; a shared habit is deleted when its last person leaves it; and failed invitation attempts are kept for one hour. Data that Google's ads software receives is kept by Google under its own policies.

Children

Quilt is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Sharing a habit asks you to confirm that you are 16 or older before you sign in. Nothing in Quilt is public: only the people in a habit you share can see it.

Your rights under the GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability.

In practice, most of these you can exercise yourself and instantly: the data is on your phone. Deleting a habit erases it; uninstalling the app erases everything; the export feature is data portability. For the purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to baltajmn@gmail.com and we will act on your request. Note that we can only identify your records if you provide the anonymous identifier or the Google Play or App Store order number, precisely because those records hold nothing that identifies you. If you have an account, you can see and correct your data (your name) and delete it (your account) in the app. What Google receives through the ads is handled by Google; you can stop it by buying Pro, by withdrawing your consent in Settings, Ad privacy options (EEA, UK and Switzerland), by resetting the advertising ID on Android, or by turning off tracking for Quilt in iOS Settings.

Legal bases: performance of a contract (delivering the purchase you made, and providing shared habits when you choose to use them), your consent for the ads in the free version (asked through Google's consent form and, on iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt, and withdrawable at any time in Settings, Ad privacy options) and our legitimate interest in preventing fraudulent restores and in acting on reports to keep shared habits safe. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. In Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

Your rights under the CCPA / CPRA

If you are a California resident: in the last twelve months we have collected the categories "identifiers" (an anonymous installation identifier and device identifiers; and, only if you share a habit with friends, your account identifier, email address and chosen name, and a notification token for your phone) and "commercial information" (your purchase history), plus, only if you share a habit, the content you choose to share (its name and the days you did it). The purpose is delivering the product you bought and, if you use it, shared habits.

We have not sold personal information for money. In the free version, Google's ads software also receives identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, the iPhone advertising identifier if you allow tracking, and your IP address), information about how you interact with the ads and the app, and approximate location derived from your IP address, so that ads can be shown and measured. California law may treat that as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising, so we do not say that nothing is shared in the free version. Quilt Pro removes the ads and with them this flow of data; resetting or deleting the advertising ID in Android settings, or refusing tracking on iPhone, limits it too. We do not knowingly sell the personal information of anyone under 16. You have the right to know, to delete, to correct, to opt out of sharing and to non-discrimination for exercising those rights; write to baltajmn@gmail.com.

Do Not Track

Do Not Track is a browser signal. Quilt's website sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising scripts, so there is nothing in it for that signal to change. In the app, the ads of the free version can use device identifiers (see "Ads (free version)" above); you control that with the consent form, with the iPhone tracking permission, with the advertising ID on Android, or by buying Pro.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the app’s release notes in the store.

Quilt («la app») está publicada por Baltasar Jiménez («nosotros»), desarrollador individual con residencia en España, que actúa como responsable del tratamiento limitado que se describe abajo.

Lo que se queda en tu dispositivo

Todo lo que creas de verdad en Quilt se guarda en local, en un fichero dentro del almacenamiento privado de la app y en un contenedor compartido que permite al widget leerlo:

  • Los hábitos que creas: nombre, emoji, color, días programados y objetivo diario.

  • Las horas de tus recordatorios.

Nada de esto se nos envía ni se envía a terceros, con una excepción que eliges tú: un hábito que compartes con amigos (ver abajo). Desinstalar la app lo borra de tu móvil.

Lo que sí sale de tu dispositivo

Compartir un hábito con amigos (cuenta opcional)

Todo lo anterior funciona sin cuenta. Solo compartir un hábito con otras personas te pide iniciar sesión, con Iniciar sesión con Apple o Google, y solo cuando abres esa parte de la app. Si no lo haces nunca, nada de esta sección te afecta.

Cuando lo usas guardamos en nuestro servidor: el identificador y el correo electrónico que Apple o Google nos dan al iniciar sesión (con Apple puedes elegir una dirección de reenvío privada), el nombre que eliges mostrar, tu código de amistad, tus amigos y las personas que bloqueas, y los reportes que envías. De cada hábito que compartes o al que te unes guardamos su nombre, emoji, horario y objetivo, las personas que están en él y, de cada una, los días que lo ha hecho en las últimas ocho semanas. Solo lo ven las personas de un hábito compartido, y ven tu nombre elegido y qué días lo hiciste. Tus demás hábitos, tu historial, tus notas, tus recordatorios y tus estadísticas se quedan en tu móvil: compartir nunca los envía.

El sistema de cuentas se comparte con Chroma, FlowTime y MoodTraker, otras apps del mismo desarrollador: si inicias sesión en varias con la misma cuenta de Apple o Google, es una sola cuenta, pero cada app guarda su propio nombre, amigos y datos, y ninguna puede ver los de otra.

Hay tolerancia cero con nombres y hábitos abusivos. Puedes reportar o bloquear a cualquiera desde la app, y un reporte nos envía un correo con los nombres de las dos personas para que actuemos en 24 horas; lo entrega Resend, Inc. como encargado del tratamiento. Consulta las condiciones de uso.

Para que Quilt te avise cuando alguien te invita a un hábito o se une al tuyo, también con la app cerrada, guardamos con tu cuenta un identificador de notificaciones de tu móvil. Se entrega a Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, en Android) o a Apple (servicio de notificaciones push de Apple, en iPhone) para hacer llegar cada aviso, junto con su texto, que incluye el nombre elegido de la persona y el nombre del hábito compartido. Puedes cambiar tu nombre, salir de un hábito, quitar a personas de un hábito que empezaste, bloquear y desbloquear, cerrar sesión y borrar tu cuenta cuando quieras desde la app (Ajustes, Amigos, Borrar cuenta; también en quilt.baltajmn.dev/delete.html). Al borrarla se eliminan al momento del servidor tu nombre, tus amigos, tus bloqueos y tu lugar en cada hábito compartido, con los días que marcaste allí. Si también usas otra app nuestra con la misma cuenta, la cuenta de esa app se conserva hasta que la borres allí.

Anuncios (versión gratis)

La versión gratis de Quilt muestra anuncios de Google AdMob: un único banner. No hay anuncios a pantalla completa. Quilt Pro, comprado una vez o al mes, quita el banner. Si ya usabas Quilt antes de que existieran los anuncios, nunca lo ves; en una instalación nueva aparece a partir del tercer día. Quilt pide a Google que solo muestre anuncios aptos para todos los públicos (clasificación de contenido G).

Cuando se muestran anuncios, el software de anuncios de Google en tu móvil recoge y recibe lo siguiente, para publicidad, analítica y prevención del fraude, y lo comparte con Google:

Quilt no le da al software de anuncios tus hábitos, tu historial ni tus ajustes: el único hábito que Quilt envía a algún sitio es uno que eliges compartir con amigos, y va a nuestro propio servidor, no a Google. Cuánto tiempo se conservan los datos

Los datos de tu dispositivo se conservan hasta que borras el hábito o desinstalas la app. Los registros de compra que guardan RevenueCat y la tienda se conservan el tiempo que esos servicios necesiten para poder restaurar compras y para cumplir sus propias obligaciones legales y fiscales. Los datos de la cuenta se conservan hasta que la borras. Los días de un hábito compartido se conservan las últimas ocho semanas y los más antiguos se borran cada noche; quien sale de un hábito compartido se lleva sus días; un hábito compartido se borra cuando sale la última persona; y los intentos fallidos de invitación se conservan una hora. Los datos que recibe el software de anuncios de Google los conserva Google con sus propias políticas.

Tus derechos según la CCPA / CPRA

Si resides en California: en los últimos doce meses hemos recogido las categorías «identificadores» (un identificador anónimo de instalación e identificadores de dispositivo; y, solo si compartes un hábito con amigos, el identificador de tu cuenta, tu correo electrónico, tu nombre elegido y un identificador de notificaciones de tu móvil) e «información comercial» (tu historial de compras), más, solo si compartes un hábito, el contenido que eliges compartir (su nombre y los días que lo hiciste). La finalidad es entregarte el producto que compraste y, si lo usas, los hábitos compartidos.

No hemos vendido información personal a cambio de dinero. En la versión gratis, el software de anuncios de Google también recibe identificadores (como el ID de publicidad de Android, el identificador de publicidad del iPhone si permites el seguimiento y tu dirección IP), información sobre cómo interactúas con los anuncios y con la app, y la ubicación aproximada que se deduce de tu IP, para poder mostrar y medir anuncios. La ley de California puede tratar eso como "compartir" para publicidad conductual entre contextos, así que no decimos que en la versión gratis no se comparta nada. Quilt Pro quita los anuncios y con ellos este flujo de datos; restablecer o borrar el ID de publicidad en los ajustes de Android, o rechazar el seguimiento en el iPhone, también lo limita. No vendemos conscientemente información personal de menores de 16 años. Do Not Track

Do Not Track es una señal del navegador. La web de Quilt no usa cookies y no tiene scripts de analítica ni de publicidad, así que no hay nada que esa señal pueda cambiar. En la app, los anuncios de la versión gratis pueden usar identificadores del dispositivo (ver "Anuncios (versión gratis)" más arriba); lo controlas con el formulario de consentimiento, con el permiso de seguimiento del iPhone, con el ID de publicidad en Android o comprando Pro.

Cambios en esta política

Si esta política cambia, la nueva versión se publicará en esta misma dirección con una fecha nueva arriba. Los cambios importantes se describirán además en las notas de la versión de la tienda.
