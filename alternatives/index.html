Habit tracker apps compared

Looking for a habit tracker app with no subscription, no ads or a one-time purchase? Here are five side by side, with what we could confirm on October 26, 2026. Loop is free. Streaks and Quilt Pro can be bought once, and HabitKit has a Lifetime plan. Habitica is free with an optional subscription. Quilt, Streaks, Loop and HabitKit have no ads; Google Play marks Habitica "Contains ads".
