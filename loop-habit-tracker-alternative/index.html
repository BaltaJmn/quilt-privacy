Loop Habit Tracker for iPhone: what you can use instead

Loop Habit Tracker is free, open source and Android only; there is no official iPhone app. If you use an iPhone, or both, Quilt is a habit tracker for Android and iPhone. If you only use Android, Loop costs nothing and has no limits, so read the table before you switch.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

Quilt fits if

  • You use an iPhone, or an iPhone and an Android phone, and want the same habit tracker on both.
  • You want each habit as a full-year grid, 52 or 53 columns by 7 rows, with the months labeled.
  • You want to share an image of your week, month or year, and mark any past day with a tap.

Loop Habit Tracker fits if

  • You only use Android and want no limits at all: unlimited habits and every feature, free.
  • You want open source code, available on F-Droid.
  • You want a habit score that forgives a few missed days, or export to CSV and SQLite.

Where Loop Habit Tracker is the better pick

  • Free with no limits

    Unlimited habits and every feature for everyone, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Quilt is free for 3 habits.

  • Open source, on F-Droid

    The code is GPL-3.0 on GitHub, the app is on Google Play and F-Droid, and volunteers keep it updated: version 2.3.2 came out on October 17, 2026. Google Play says it collects no data.

  • A habit score that forgives, and many users

    The habit score is built so that a few missed days after a long streak do not wipe out your progress, and Google Play shows more than 5 million installs. Quilt uses skipped days instead, a different idea.

Questions

Is there a Loop Habit Tracker for iPhone?

No. Its README and its website link only to Google Play and F-Droid, so there is no official iPhone app (checked October 26, 2026). A search on the App Store finds an app with a similar name; it is not the Android Loop Habit Tracker. Quilt is a different app that runs on iPhone and Android.

Is Loop Habit Tracker free?

Yes, completely. Its README says all features are available to all users and there are no in-app purchases, and it has no ads. Quilt is free for 3 habits; Pro is $1.99 once or $0.99 a month.

Is Loop Habit Tracker open source?

Yes. The code is under the GPL-3.0 licence on GitHub, and the app is also on F-Droid.

Can I move my Loop data to Quilt?

Not automatically. Loop exports CSV and SQLite files; Quilt imports only its own JSON backup and cannot read other apps' files. You can fill in past days by tapping them on the year grid. The two apps do not sync, so if you use both, their data stays apart.

Try Quilt for a week

Add the habits you track now and tick a week. If it is not for you, there is no account to close and nothing to cancel.
