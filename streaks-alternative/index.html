A Streaks alternative for Android and iPhone

Streaks is a habit tracker for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple Vision Pro; there is no Android version. It costs $5.99 once, with no subscription, no ads and no account. Quilt is an option if you use Android, or both, or would rather start free. Here is how the two compare.

Google Play App Store

Checked on October 26, 2026

Quilt fits if

  • You use Android, or Android and iPhone, and want the same habit tracker on both.
  • You want each habit as a full-year grid, and skipped days that do not break a streak.
  • You would rather start free: 3 habits, every widget and reminder and the full export cost nothing.

Streaks fits if

  • You live on Apple devices and want Apple Health to complete tasks for you.
  • You want an Apple Watch app, a Mac app and iCloud sync.
  • You want more ways to set a schedule: N times a day, every N days, or goals counted by week, month or year.

Where Streaks is the better pick

  • Apple Health, Watch and more

    Apple Health completes tasks for you (steps, water, mindful minutes, Activity rings), and there are Apple Watch, Mac and Vision Pro apps, Live Activities and timed tasks. Quilt has no Health integration and no Watch app.

  • iCloud sync and sharing

    Your tasks sync between your Apple devices, and you can share tasks through iCloud. Quilt does not sync between devices.

  • Richer rules, one price

    Many ways to say when a task is due, week, month and year goals, and one $5.99 purchase that covers every Apple device. Its site says it won an Apple Design Award in 2016.

Questions

Is there a Streaks for Android?

No. Streaks lists iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Mac and Apple Vision Pro as the devices it works on, and does not mention Android. Quilt is a habit tracker for Android and iPhone, so it is an option if you want one app on both.

Is Streaks free?

No. On October 26, 2026 the US App Store price was $5.99, paid once, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. Quilt is free for 3 habits, every widget, every reminder and the full export; Pro adds unlimited habits and the full color palette, $1.99 once or $0.99 a month. They are not like for like: Streaks has no free tier, and its price covers up to 24 tasks on every Apple device.

Is Streaks worth it?

If you use Apple Health, an Apple Watch or a Mac, or want iCloud sync, those are things Streaks does and Quilt does not. If what you want is a full-year grid for each habit, free widgets, Android as well as iPhone and a free start, that is what Quilt is built around.

Can I move my Streaks data to Quilt?

Not automatically. Streaks exports a CSV and a .streaks backup, but Quilt imports only its own JSON backup and cannot read other apps' files. To bring a past year over, tap the days on Quilt's year grid.

Try Quilt for a week

Add the habits you track now and tick a week. If it is not for you, there is no account to close and nothing to cancel.
