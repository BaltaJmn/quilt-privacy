How to use a habit tracker

  1. Pick two or three habits

    Small enough to do on a bad day. Write them on the sheet, one per row or grid.

  2. Fill a square each evening

    Same time every day. Keep the sheet where you will see it: on the fridge, by the bed.

  3. A dot for a skipped day

    Ill, travelling, a planned rest: a dot says you skipped it on purpose. It does not break the streak.

  4. Look back once a month

    Count the squares. Empty runs show what got in the way; the full weeks show what worked.
